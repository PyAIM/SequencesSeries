# SequencesSeries
repository for sequences and series tutorial website

This website is intended to help college students at the undergraduate level with the comprehension and practice of the concepts of sequences, series, power series, Maclaurin and Taylon Series, and the convergence and divergence of sequences, series, and power series. These topics are typically taught in a 2nd semester of calculus for students pursuing engineering and sciences degree programs. The website focuses on these particular topics because they are typically the concepts students find the most challenging to grasp in such a course. The author who has taught calculus at the college level for over 23 years hopes that this website can provide an additional resource for teachers and students alike to support course materials and assist students with their learning.

Author: Professor Moez Ben-Azzouz
