About Calculus Explorer

Learn more about our mission and the people behind this educational resource

Our Mission

Calculus Explorer is designed to help college students master sequences, series, and power series concepts through clear explanations and interactive examples.

We believe that mathematics should be accessible to everyone, and our goal is to create resources that make complex calculus concepts easier to understand through visual learning, interactive examples, and comprehensive explanations.

About the Author

Author: Moez Ben-Azzouz

Professor Moez Ben-Azzouz created Calculus Explorer with the vision of providing college students with a comprehensive resource for understanding sequences and series. With a passion for mathematics education, Professor Ben-Azzouz developed this platform to make calculus concepts more accessible through interactive visualizations and clear explanations.

Many thanks to my colleague Professor Len Ruth for his valuable edits and feedback.

How to Use This Resource

Calculus Explorer can be used in several ways:

  • As a learning companion: Work through the topics sequentially to build your understanding from the ground up
  • As a reference guide: Jump directly to specific topics when you need clarification
  • As a practice tool: Use the interactive examples and exercises to test your knowledge

We recommend starting with the Sequences section if you're new to these concepts, and then progressing through Series, Power Series, and Taylor Series.
